Project Overview

Developed an AI-assisted visualization and compositing workflow for the NIBSS headquarters presentation, translating architectural concepts into a cohesive visual narrative that connected spatial design, corporate identity, and stakeholder communication.

Process 01

1. Ideation & Conceptual Visualization We initiated the project by translating the lead architect’s raw design directions into compelling visual targets. Leveraging a hybrid workflow of AI generation and advanced Photoshop compositing, we developed stylized, sketch-like renders. This aesthetic choice was highly intentional, it captured the grand scale and mood of the building while signalling to the client that this was a collaborative, evolving concept rather than a rigid, finalized structure.

Process 02

Narrative & Deck Architecture The visual language established in those initial renders served as the foundation for the entire pitch. Rather than simply dropping images into a standard template, our team unravelled the core aesthetic themes from the visualizations to dictate the flow, layout, and emotional resonance of the presentation. We crafted the copywriting to map directly to the imagery, ensuring the architectural narrative spoke the client's corporate language.

Process 03

Brand Synthesis With the visual and narrative direction established, we integrated the existing client brand assets into the fold. These assets acted as a guiding voice for our graphic design team, who carefully blended them with the fresh architectural inspiration. This ensured the pitch felt both innovative in its structural design and distinctly tailored to the NIBSS identity.

Process 04

Iteration & Refinement The final phase involved rigorous, collaborative cycles of implementation and revision. Our cross-functional team fine-tuned the balance between the bold architectural proposal and the refined editorial layout, resulting in a cohesive, premium pitch deck ready for high-level client review.
